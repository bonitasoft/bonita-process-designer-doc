= User guide
:description: Learn how to create, edit, collaborate on, and export BPMN diagrams in Bonita Process Designer.

{description}

This guide covers everything you do day to day in {productName}: building and managing diagrams, working with the BPMN editor, collaborating with your team in real time, generating diagrams with AI, simulating a process, and exporting your work for use in Bonita Studio.

What you can do depends on your role and the groups you belong to. Readers can view and comment, Creators can build and edit diagrams in their groups, and Admins have full access. See xref:administration:permissions-and-roles.adoc[Roles and permissions] for the full rights matrix.
