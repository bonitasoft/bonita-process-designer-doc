= Data retention and privacy
:description: What {productName} deletes automatically and when, what it keeps until someone deletes it, what product analytics sends when an Admin turns it on, what the AI prompt-retention consent covers, and which sites can display the application in a frame.

{description}

Most of this page is for administrators and for the people who answer privacy questionnaires about your installation. The automatic deletions are set by the operator who installs {productNameShort}, with environment variables. The two consents are set by an Admin, in xref:administration:application-settings.adoc[Application settings].

[#automatic-deletion]
== What is deleted automatically

{productNameShort} deletes some data on its own, on a schedule. All of it relates to AI generation and to sign-in sessions.

[cols="2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Data | When it is deleted | Setting (default)

| The result of an AI job: the process generated or modified by AI, or the analysis text
| 24 hours after the job succeeded. The job record itself is kept.
| `AI_JOB_RESULT_TTL_HOURS` (`24`)

| The instruction typed for an AI *Modify*, and the question typed for an AI *Analyze*
| As soon as the job finishes, whether it succeeded or failed.
| Not configurable

| The record of a finished AI job: its identifiers, kind, status, timings, AI provider and model, and error code
| 30 days after the job was submitted.
| `AI_JOB_RETENTION_DAYS` (`30`)

| The AI usage events that the AI quota counts
| 90 days after they were recorded.
| `AI_USAGE_RETENTION_DAYS` (`90`)

| Expired sign-in sessions
| Every minute. A session expires after 30 minutes without activity.
| `SESSION_CLEANUP_CRON` (every minute)
|===

The text of an AI *Generate* prompt is not stored by {productNameShort} at all: it is sent to the AI service and is not kept in the database. What the AI service keeps is covered by the <<ai-prompt-retention,AI prompt-retention consent>>.

[#schedule]
=== When the deletions run

* AI job results are cleared by a task that runs every hour. Its first run is one hour after the application starts.
* AI job records and AI usage events are deleted by a task that runs once a day. Its first run is *24 hours after the application starts*, and the next one 24 hours after the previous one ends. An instance restarted more often than once a day therefore never runs it.
* Expired sessions are removed every minute.

[#retention-settings]
=== Change the retention periods

The operator sets these variables in the application's environment, like the other settings in the xref:installation:configuration.adoc[Configuration reference], and restarts the application.

* `DATA_RETENTION_ENABLED=false` turns off the daily task: AI job records and AI usage events are then kept indefinitely. It does not stop the hourly clearing of AI job results, nor the removal of expired sessions.
* Keep `AI_USAGE_RETENTION_DAYS` at `30` or more. The AI quota counts usage over the last 30 days, so a shorter value deletes events the quota still counts, and a group can then use more than its limit. The application logs a warning at startup when the value is below 30.

To check how an instance is set, download a xref:administration:support-diagnostics.adoc[diagnostic archive]: its configuration section shows the value of each of these variables, or says that the built-in default applies.

[#kept]
== What is kept until someone deletes it

Everything else stays in the database until a user or an Admin deletes it. There is no automatic deletion, and no age limit, for:

* diagrams and their folders, labels and comments;
* the versions of each diagram, automatic and manual;
* the import logs of imported diagrams;
* users and groups;
* the history of the consent changes — who turned product analytics or the AI prompt-retention consent on or off, and when.

The application logs are not in the database. They rotate and are bounded in size, as described in xref:administration:logging.adoc[Logs and log levels]. Database backups that you take yourself follow your own retention rules.

[#product-analytics]
== Product analytics

{productNameShort} can send usage events to PostHog, the analytics service Ofelia uses to understand how the product is used. It is *off by default*: nothing is sent until an Admin turns it on.

[#analytics-toggle]
=== Turn product analytics on or off

. Open the *Admin* panel, select the *Settings* tab, and go to the *Product Analytics* section.
. Turn on the toggle.
. Read the clause in the *Enable product analytics?* dialog, tick the acknowledgment box, and select *Enable analytics*.

To stop, turn the toggle off and confirm with *Disable analytics*. The section shows when the setting was last changed, and by whom.

The change applies at once in your own browser. Other users' browsers pick it up when they load a page, once their copy of the setting has expired: a browser keeps it for up to five minutes. A page that stays open keeps the setting it was loaded with.

[#analytics-content]
=== What is sent

When product analytics is on, each user's browser sends events to PostHog (`eu.posthog.com`), for example when a diagram is opened, saved, exported or imported, or when an AI generation starts. An event carries:

* counts and technical values, such as a number of elements, a file format or a duration;
* identifiers (UUIDs), such as the diagram and the user;
* the user's role;
* the company name of the installation, as shown in the *AI Configuration* section of xref:administration:application-settings.adoc#ai-configuration[Application settings];
* technical information about the browser: browser, operating system and screen.

Page addresses are reduced to the page type: the parts that could carry a name or a search term are removed.

[#analytics-never]
=== What is never sent

Product analytics never receives AI prompts or excerpts of them, AI error messages, diagram titles or diagram content, folder names, search terms, or user names. The browser enforces this with a list of allowed events and properties: anything not on the list is dropped before it leaves the browser. Automatic click capture, session replay, heatmaps, surveys and feature flags are turned off.

Product analytics is independent of AI generation: turning it on or off changes nothing for AI.

[#ai-prompt-retention]
== AI prompt-retention consent

When a user generates, modifies or analyzes a process with AI, the prompt and the resulting diagram are sent to the AI service, which calls the AI provider whose API key you configured. The AI prompt-retention consent is the Admin's agreement that the AI service stores them.

The consent is part of the *AI Configuration* section of xref:administration:application-settings.adoc#ai-configuration[Application settings]. Its clause says that stored data is kept for up to 2 years and used solely for product improvement, quality monitoring and debugging. That storage happens on the AI service, outside your installation.

* *Saving an API key needs this consent.* In the *AI Configuration* section, the button that saves a provider API key stays disabled until the consent box is ticked.
* *Revoking the consent* (*Revoke consent*) disables AI generation for the whole installation. The API keys stay configured.
* *To give consent again*, tick the box and save a provider API key.

The consent is independent of product analytics.

[#framing]
== Framing policy

{productNameShort} pages can be displayed inside a frame only by {productNameShort} itself, which the document tabs of the editor need. No other site can embed the application in a frame: every page is sent with `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN` and `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'self'`. This protects users against a hostile page that would show the application inside its own to trick them into clicking.

As a consequence, you cannot embed {productNameShort} in a portal or an intranet page served from another address.
