= Administration
:description: Administer {productName}: manage roles and permissions, users, groups, application settings, AI quota, and the license, read the application logs, and download a diagnostic archive for Support.

{description}

The administration tasks below let you control who can access {productName}, how access is scoped to groups, and how the application behaves across your organization.

[NOTE]
====
All administration tasks require the *Admin* role. Users with the *Creator* or *Reader* role do not see the administration panel and cannot perform these operations.
====

The admin panel has five tabs: *Labels*, *Groups*, *Users*, *Settings* and *Support*.
