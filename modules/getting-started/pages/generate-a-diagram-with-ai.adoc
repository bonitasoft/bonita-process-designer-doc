= Generate a diagram with AI
:description: Describe a business process in plain language and let {productName} generate a BPMN 2.0 diagram for you to review and refine.

{description}

Instead of dragging every element onto the canvas yourself, you can describe a process in natural language and let {productName} build a first draft for you. AI generation is the fastest way to go from an idea to a working diagram you can then edit by hand.

[NOTE]
====
AI generation is only available when an administrator has *registered the installation* and *configured AI* for it. See xref:installation:first-time-setup.adoc[First-time setup] and xref:administration:ai-quota.adoc[Manage AI quota].
====

== Before you start

To generate a diagram with AI you need:

* The *Creator* or *Admin* role in the group you are working in. Readers cannot create or generate diagrams.
* AI enabled for the installation (your administrator handles this).
* Available xref:administration:ai-quota.adoc[group quota] — each group may have a limited number of AI requests over a rolling 30-day window. Generation is refused once the group reaches its cap.

== Start a generation

You choose AI in the *Create New Diagram* wizard, at step 2, and describe the process at step 3.

. On the Home page, click *Create New Diagram*.
. In step 1, *Title*, enter the *Diagram Title*, choose the *Group* and, if your group has folders, the *Folder*. Click *Next*.
. In step 2, *How would you like to start?*, choose *Generate with AI* (*Describe in plain language*).
+
image::create-wizard-step2.png["Step 2 of the Create New Diagram wizard, with three choices: From Scratch, Generate with AI and Import BPMN",800]
. In step 3, *Describe your process*, type the description of the process in *Process Description*. Be specific about the steps, who performs them, and the decision points. The step shows *Tips for best results*.
+
[TIP]
====
Write the process as you would explain it to a colleague — for example: _"An employee submits a leave request. Their manager reviews it. If approved, the request goes to HR for processing; if rejected, it returns to the employee for revision."_

Descriptions must be written in English. Very complex processes may not generate accurately — generate a first draft, then refine it by hand.
====
. Click *Generate*.

The diagram is created, the editor opens, and generation starts on its own with the default AI provider.

[NOTE]
====
If AI is not available for the installation, the diagram is still created, and the editor opens on the *Welcome to your new diagram!* dialog. There, *Generate with AI* is greyed out and reads *Ask an administrator to configure AI* (no AI provider is configured) or *AI generation is currently unavailable* (the AI service cannot be reached right now). Choose *Start from scratch* to keep the empty diagram, or *Cancel and return to home*, which deletes it.
====

You can also generate a diagram from inside the editor, with the *Generate* tab of the AI panel. There you can pick an AI provider for that one request, and a character counter shows how much room you have left (500 characters by default). See xref:user-guide:ai-generation.adoc[AI diagram generation].

== Watch the progress

Generation runs in the background, so it keeps going even if you close the tab or briefly lose your connection. A progress overlay shows the current phase as the diagram is built:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Phase | What is happening
| Queued | Your request is waiting for a free slot.
| Planning | The structure of the process is being worked out.
| Generating | The BPMN diagram is being produced (this is the longest phase).
| Validating | The generated diagram is being checked.
| Finalizing | Layout is applied and the diagram is finished.
|===

The progress bar advances as each phase is reached. When the remaining time can be estimated it fills smoothly; otherwise it shows an indeterminate pulse. Select *Continue working* to dismiss the overlay without cancelling — the generation finishes in the background and loads when it is ready.

[NOTE]
====
If you reload the editor while a generation is still running, {productName} reattaches to it automatically and resumes showing progress. A generation that finished while the tab was closed is applied when you open the diagram again in the same browser, as long as its result has not expired (24 hours by default).
====

== Review the result

When generation succeeds, the diagram appears on the canvas, automatically zoomed to fit, and a success notification appears. You now have a normal editable diagram.

Review it before you export it to Bonita Studio: a generated diagram is placed on the canvas as the AI produced it. Unlike a file import, it does not go through the element conversion and does not produce entries in the *Import Logs* tab. See xref:user-guide:bpmn-elements.adoc[Supported BPMN elements] for the elements Bonita Studio accepts.

== Refine the diagram

The generated diagram is a starting point. From here you can:

* Edit it directly on the canvas — see xref:create-your-first-diagram.adoc[Create your first diagram] and xref:user-guide:editing-diagrams.adoc[Edit a diagram].
* Ask the AI to *modify* the current diagram with a follow-up instruction (for example, "Add a manager approval step after the first task"), or *analyze* it to get a plain-language summary. These actions are in the *Modify* and *Analyze* tabs of the AI panel. A modify request counts against your group quota; an analyze request does not.

While a generation or a modification runs, the other people editing the diagram with you are paused until it finishes. See xref:user-guide:real-time-collaboration.adoc[Collaborate in real time].

== If generation is unavailable or fails

* *AI option greyed out* — the installation is not registered or AI is not configured, or the AI service cannot be reached right now. Contact your administrator.
* *Quota exceeded* — the message reads *Your group has reached its AI generation quota for the last 30 days. Ask an administrator to raise the limit.* Wait for usage to age out of the window or ask an administrator to raise the xref:administration:ai-quota.adoc[group quota].
* *Generation failed or the service is unreachable* — try again. When the failure happened on the server or on the AI service, the notification shows a *Support ID* with a *Copy* button: quote it when you contact your administrator or Bonitasoft Support. The welcome dialog opens again so that you can retry or start from scratch.
