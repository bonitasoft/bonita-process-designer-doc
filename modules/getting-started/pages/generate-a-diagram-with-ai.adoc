= Generate a diagram with AI
:description: Describe a business process in plain language and let {productName} generate a BPMN 2.0 diagram for you to review and refine.

{description}

Instead of dragging every element onto the canvas yourself, you can describe a process in natural language and let {productName} build a first draft for you. AI generation is the fastest way to go from an idea to a working diagram you can then edit by hand.

== Before you start

To generate a diagram with AI you need:

* The *Creator* or *Admin* role. A Creator works in their own groups. Readers cannot create or generate diagrams.
* AI enabled for the installation: an administrator has *registered the installation* and *configured AI*. See xref:installation:first-time-setup.adoc[First-time setup].
* Available xref:administration:ai-quota.adoc[group quota] — each group may have a limited number of AI requests over a rolling 30-day window. Generation is refused once the group reaches its cap.

== Start a generation

You choose AI in the *Create New Diagram* wizard, and describe the process on its last screen.

. On the Home page, click *Create New Diagram*.
. On the first screen, enter the *Diagram Title*, choose the *Group* and, if your group has folders, the *Folder*, then click *Next*.
. On the *How would you like to start?* screen, choose *Generate with AI* (*Describe in plain language*).
+
image::create-wizard-step2.png["Step 2 of the Create New Diagram wizard, with three choices: From Scratch, Generate with AI and Import BPMN"]
. On the *Describe your process* screen, type the description of the process in *Process Description*. Be specific about the steps, who performs them, and the decision points.
+
[TIP]
====
Write the process as you would explain it to a colleague — for example: _"An employee submits a leave request. Their manager reviews it. If approved, the request goes to HR for processing; if rejected, it returns to the employee for revision."_

Descriptions must be written in English. Very complex processes may not generate accurately — generate a first draft, then refine it by hand.
====
. Click *Generate*.

The diagram is created, the editor opens, and generation starts on its own. A progress overlay follows the generation phases; click *Continue working* to close it while the generation goes on in the background. See xref:user-guide:ai-generation.adoc#async-progress[How generation runs].

If AI turns out to be unavailable for the installation, the diagram is still created, and the editor opens on the *Welcome to your new diagram!* dialog, with *Generate with AI* greyed out. Choose *Start from scratch* to keep the empty diagram, or *Cancel and return to home*, which deletes it, and contact your administrator.

You can also generate from inside the editor, with the *Generate* tab of the AI panel. See xref:user-guide:ai-generation.adoc#generate-from-text[Generate a diagram from text].

== Review and refine the result

When generation succeeds, the diagram appears on the canvas, automatically zoomed to fit. You now have a normal editable diagram.

Review it before you export it to Bonita Studio: a generated diagram is placed on the canvas as the AI produced it, without the element conversion that a file import applies. See xref:user-guide:bpmn-elements.adoc[Supported BPMN elements] for the elements Bonita Studio accepts.

From here you can:

* Edit it directly on the canvas — see xref:create-your-first-diagram.adoc[Create your first diagram] and xref:user-guide:editing-diagrams.adoc[Edit a diagram].
* Ask the AI to *modify* the diagram with a follow-up instruction (for example, "Add a manager approval step after the first task"), or to *analyze* it, from the *Modify* and *Analyze* tabs of the AI panel. See xref:user-guide:ai-generation.adoc#modify-analyze[Modify and analyze an existing diagram].

== If generation fails

Try again: most failures are temporary. When the failure happened on the server or on the AI service, the message shows a *Support ID* with a *Copy* button: quote it when you contact your administrator or xref:ROOT:get-support.adoc#ai-support-id[Ofelia Support]. If your group has used its quota, wait for usage to age out of the 30-day window or ask an administrator to raise the xref:administration:ai-quota.adoc[group quota].

For the full reference — progress, resuming after a reload, the provider picker, failures and quota — see xref:user-guide:ai-generation.adoc[AI diagram generation].
