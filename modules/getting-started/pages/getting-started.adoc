= Getting started
:description: Everything you need to begin creating BPMN diagrams with Bonita Process Designer, from a quick tour of the interface to building or generating your first diagram.

{description}

== What you need

Before you start, make sure you have:

* *A running instance* of {productName}. If no instance is available yet, ask your administrator, or see the operator guide to xref:installation:installation.adoc[install and configure one].
* *An account* on that instance, with a username and password. Your administrator creates accounts and may share an initial password that you change on first login.
* *A role*. Your role determines what you can do:
+
** *Reader* — view diagrams, folders and comments in your groups, add comments, export and simulate diagrams.
** *Creator* — create diagrams and folders in your groups, and edit, move or delete any diagram or folder in those groups.
** *Admin* — full access across all groups, plus user, group, and settings management.

+
Access is scoped to *groups*: you see and work on the diagrams and folders that belong to the groups you are a member of. For the full breakdown, see xref:administration:permissions-and-roles.adoc[Roles and permissions].

[NOTE]
====
Generating a diagram with AI also requires the instance to be registered and AI to be configured by an administrator, and is subject to a per-group quota. See xref:getting-started:generate-a-diagram-with-ai.adoc[Generate a diagram with AI].
====

== Where to start

After you sign in, you land on the home page, at its top level, *Home*. From there:

* To create a diagram, select *Create New Diagram*, available to Creators and Admins. The wizard asks for a title, a group and, when the group has folders, the folder to file the diagram in; then you start from scratch, generate the diagram with AI, or import a BPMN file.
* To find a diagram, open a folder, switch to the *Tree* view, or type in the search field of the header.
* To open a diagram, select its card. The editor opens.
* If you are an Admin setting up a new installation, start with xref:installation:first-time-setup.adoc[First-time setup].
* If your installation was just upgraded to 1.1, every existing diagram is at *Home*: see xref:how-tos:organize-existing-processes-into-folders.adoc[Organize existing processes into folders].
